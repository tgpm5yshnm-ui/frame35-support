Frequently asked

How does Frame35 work?
Pick or take a photo, tap Improve, and Frame35 turns it into a 35mm film-style portrait in seconds. You can also combine a few photos into one scene.

What does it cost?
Improving a photo is a $0.99 in-app purchase, which includes one free re-try if you're not happy with the result.

Where are my improved photos?
Every improved photo is saved automatically in the app's gallery. You can also save any of them to your device's Photos library, or share them.

I was charged but got no photo.
Re-open the app and tap Improve again — your purchase is preserved, so you won't be charged twice. If it keeps happening, message us above.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Frame35 ("we", "the app") is a photo-enhancement app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We've kept it short because the app collects very little.

What we handle

The only data the app sends off your device is the photo you choose to improve or combine. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name, email, or any personal identifiers, and we do not track you.

How your photos are processed

When you tap Improve or Combine, your selected photo is sent — over an encrypted connection — to a third-party AI image-processing service that generates the enhanced result and returns it to your device. Your photo is used only to produce that result.

These processing providers temporarily store the uploaded photo and the generated result on their servers (by default for about 7 days), after which the files automatically expire and are deleted. Frame35 does not keep copies of your photos on our own servers.

Any third party we use to process your photo is bound by its own privacy and data-processing terms, which require it to protect your data to a standard equivalent to this policy, to process your photo only to perform the enhancement you requested, and not to use it to identify you.

Photos you keep

Improved photos are saved automatically in the app's in-app gallery, which is stored locally on your device. If you tap Save, a copy is also added to your device's Photos library. You can delete any photo from the in-app gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes the in-app gallery.

Your choices and data deletion

You are in control of your data:

  • Delete any photo from the in-app gallery at any time to remove it from your device.
  • Copies held by the processing provider expire automatically (about 7 days) — no action needed.
  • To request deletion of your data, withdraw your consent, or ask any privacy question, contact us using the support form at the top of this page and we will respond.
  • You can stop all off-device processing at any time by not using the Improve or Combine features, or by uninstalling the app.

Purchases

Payments for the in-app purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or logins.
  • No analytics, advertising, or marketing SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, or microphone access.

Children

Frame35 is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we will update the date shown above. Use of Frame35 is also subject to Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
